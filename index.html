Who We Are

A Foundation Rooted in Compassion & Action

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. Operating across India, we address critical societal challenges through structured programs and a growing network of passionate volunteers.

Our mission is to serve communities with compassion. Our vision is to create opportunities for every underprivileged person to grow with dignity and respect.

Section 8 Registered ISO 9001:2015 80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered NITI Aayog
